How it works

Three steps. No setup.

1

Say your task

By voice or text: “I need to write my report.”

2

Get one tiny step

2 to 10 minutes. A timer. The right tool opens.

3

Nox stays with you

It brings you back, and celebrates each step.

It works in three steps. One: you say your task, with your voice or with text. Two: Nox gives you one tiny step, two to ten minutes. It starts a timer and opens the right tool, like Google Docs. Three: Nox stays with you. If you drift, it brings you back. When you finish a step, it is happy for you.
