The problem

For people with ADHD, the hard part is not the work.

Starting

You know what to do. You just can’t begin.

Staying on it

One notification, and the task is gone.

Coming back

After a break: “Wait… where was I?”

And every app waits for you to open it.

For people with ADHD, the hard part is not the work. It is starting. It is staying on the task. And it is coming back after a break. The problem: every app waits for you. A to-do list does nothing if you don’t open it.
