You'll see Nox:
🗣 introduce itself out loud and get to know you in under a minute
🪜 turn "I need to write my internship report" into one tiny first step
📄 open Google Docs for you, and start the clock
📺 notice, on its own, that you drifted to a cat video on YouTube… and gently bring you back
⭐ celebrate when the step is done, and level you up

Built this weekend for the OpenAI DevDay Community Hackathon in Paris:
→ a working Windows app with real-time OpenAI voice

Nox makes the first move in front of the computer. That's what ADHD brains need. It's also what anyone needs in front of a tax form, a screen they can barely read, or a computer they never learned to use.

Next:
