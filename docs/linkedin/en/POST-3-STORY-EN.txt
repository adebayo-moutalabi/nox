Lea is 24. She was diagnosed with ADHD three years ago.
She's bright, creative… and every day, starting a task feels like climbing a wall. 🧗‍♀️

To-do lists, planners, alarms, five different apps: she tried them all.
They all have the same problem: 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝘆 𝘄𝗮𝗶𝘁 𝗳𝗼𝗿 𝘆𝗼𝘂.

So we built Nox, a little robot that lives on your desktop and 𝗺𝗮𝗸𝗲𝘀 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗳𝗶𝗿𝘀𝘁 𝗺𝗼𝘃𝗲. 🤖

In this short story, you'll see Nox:
🪜 turn a scary report into one tiny 5-minute step
🫶 notice the cat video… and bring Lea back, kindly
📅 remind her of a call she totally forgot
📬 flag the email that matters
⭐ celebrate every step she finishes

And then her grandma Rose calls about a 12-page tax form. 🧾
That's what's coming next: Nox reading forms out loud, explaining every box in simple words, and filling them in with her. She checks it. She sends it. 💛

Nox started with ADHD. Next, it's for everyone the computer leaves behind: paperwork, low vision, older people.

Free for Windows today (English & French), built in one weekend at the OpenAI DevDay Community Hackathon in Paris.
