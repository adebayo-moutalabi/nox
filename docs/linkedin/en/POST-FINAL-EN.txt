Meet Nox 🤖
A tiny robot that lives on your desktop and talks to you first.

Here's what a day with Nox looks like:

🎙 You say: "I need to write my report."
🪜 Nox breaks it into tiny steps. You only see the next one: "Open the doc."
⏱ A timer ring starts. Time stops being invisible.
📺 You drift to YouTube? A gentle nudge: "Let's keep that for your break."
☕ You leave for 20 minutes? When you're back: "You were at: write the intro."
🧠 It remembers you. What blocks you, when your energy peaks, what helps.

No guilt. No streaks to lose. 𝗝𝘂𝘀𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲 𝗻𝗲𝘅𝘁 𝘀𝗺𝗮𝗹𝗹 𝘀𝘁𝗲𝗽.

Not magic: the onboarding and the 7 built-in methods (Pomodoro, body doubling, the 5-minute rule, "if… then…" plans) come straight from ADHD and executive-function research.
